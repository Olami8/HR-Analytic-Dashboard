# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition to understand why employees leave an organization, identify workforce segments with higher attrition rates, and provide actionable recommendations to improve employee retention.

Using Microsoft Power BI, I developed an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard that transforms employee data into meaningful insights to support strategic HR decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition can affect organizational productivity, workforce stability, recruitment costs, and overall business performance.

The central business question guiding this project is:

**Why are employees leaving the organization, which employees are most at risk of leaving, and what can HR management do to improve retention?**

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition and retention performance.
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition rates.
- Examine the relationship between workplace conditions and employee attrition.
- Explore demographic and career-related attrition patterns.
- Establish a hypothetical 10% attrition target to evaluate retention performance.
- Provide actionable recommendations to support employee retention.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboard development.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** KPI calculations, attrition analysis, and dynamic management insights.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Dataset review and preparation.
- **GitHub:** Project documentation and version-controlled project storage.

## 🧹 Data Cleaning and Preparation

The dataset was prepared using Power Query. Key activities included:

- Removed exact duplicate records.
- Standardized inconsistent Business Travel category spelling.
- Removed constant and unnecessary columns.
- Reviewed missing values and data types.
- Validated key columns and checked data quality.
- Retained records with differing employee attributes rather than treating them as exact duplicates.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

The dashboard consists of four interactive pages:

### 1. HR Overview
Provides a high-level view of workforce performance through key performance indicators, including total employee records, employees who left, employees who stayed, and overall attrition rate.

It also highlights attrition patterns across departments, age groups, and job roles.

### 2. Attrition Drivers
Examines workplace factors associated with employee attrition, including:

- Overtime
- Job Satisfaction
- Work-Life Balance
- Business Travel
- Environment Satisfaction
- Relationship Satisfaction

### 3. Workforce & Career Analysis
Explores attrition patterns across employee demographics and career characteristics, including:

- Job Role
- Salary Slab
- Marital Status
- Gender
- Distance From Home

### 4. Management Insights & Recommendations
Presents dynamic management insights, key employee retention priorities, and practical HR recommendations based on the analysis.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The analysis revealed the following findings:

1. **Department Attrition:** The Sales department recorded the highest attrition rate.
2. **Job Role:** Laboratory Technicians showed a higher attrition rate than Human Resources.
3. **Age Group:** Employees aged 18–25 recorded the highest attrition.
4. **Salary Slab:** Employees earning 10+ LPA recorded an attrition rate of 19.2%.
5. **Overtime:** Employees working overtime had a 31.0% attrition rate compared with 10.3% among employees who did not.
6. **Job Satisfaction:** Employees with the lowest job satisfaction rating recorded a 23.1% attrition rate.
7. **Work-Life Balance:** Employees with the lowest work-life balance rating recorded a 30.9% attrition rate.
8. **Business Travel:** Employees who travelled frequently recorded a 24.3% attrition rate.
9. **Distance From Home:** Employees in the 20 km distance band recorded a 25.4% attrition rate.
10. **Marital Status:** Single employees recorded an attrition rate of 21.6%.

## 💡 Management Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

- **Workload Management:** Review overtime schedules and redistribute workloads to reduce employee pressure.
- **Employee Engagement:** Conduct regular employee surveys and feedback sessions to identify dissatisfaction early.
- **Career Development:** Strengthen training, mentorship, promotion opportunities, and career progression pathways.
- **Targeted Retention:** Prioritize retention initiatives in departments and job roles with higher attrition.
- **Employee Wellbeing:** Improve work-life balance and workplace satisfaction.
- **Performance Monitoring:** Track attrition against the hypothetical 10% target and regularly evaluate retention strategies.

*Note: The identified patterns represent associations in the dataset and do not establish causation.*
